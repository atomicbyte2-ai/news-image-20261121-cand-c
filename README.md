"THE WORLD IN ONE IMAGE" — a cream broadsheet front page summing up Saturday 21 November 2026 in 38 stories, all first reported that day. A masthead, a giant serif date, a headline strip, a day-in-numbers strip (12 killed · 82 wounded · 900,000 AI chips · $7.25B Bayer deal · 20 commanders · 500 migrants), a region bar whose counts sum exactly to 38 (Europe 11 · Asia 7 · Middle East 3 · Africa 2 · Americas 11 · Oceania 1 · Global 3), four big illustrated leads in a row (COP31 overtime, Kryvyi Rih strikes, 900,000 AI chips cleared, Bayer's $7.25B settlement), nine small illustrated cards, 25 numbered one-liners, a dotted world map with numbered dots, and a DATE CHECK box listing the 20 November stories deliberately left out. Every headline and number is exact programmatic text; all illustrations are flat vector-style drawings made with Python PIL.

## Run it

```
python3 build_entry.py
```
